================================================================================
CHANGES MADE TO THE ORIGINAL IMAGES
================================================================================

Required disclosure under the "indicate if changes were made" clause:

  1. Downscaled so the longest side is at most 1024 px (aspect ratio preserved,
     Lanczos resampling). Originals are 1592x1728 to 2232x1727.
  2. Re-encoded as progressive JPEG at quality 85.
  3. EXIF and ICC metadata stripped.
  4. Renamed and reorganised into grade / eye folders.
  5. Fields re-labelled as "macula" and "disc" based on measured optic-disc
     position (see README.md). No pixel content was altered by this step.

No cropping, colour correction, enhancement, annotation or retouching was
applied. The retinal content is unmodified.
